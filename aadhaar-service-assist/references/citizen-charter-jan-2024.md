## 1. Introduction

UIDAI has been set up for providing unique identification numbers (UID), called "Aadhaar", to all residents of India. This identity is (a) robust enough to eliminate duplication and fake identities and (b) capable of being verified and authenticated in a simple, cost-effective manner.

## 2. Our Vision

The Vision of UIDAI is to empower residents of India with a unique identity and a digital platform to enable authentication anytime, anywhere.

## 3. Mission Statement

- To facilitate the provision of good governance, efficient, transparent and targeted delivery of subsidies, benefits and services, the expenditure for which is incurred from the Consolidated Fund of India or Consolidated Fund of the State, to individuals residing in India through assigning of unique identity numbers.
- To develop policy, procedure and system for issuing Aadhaar number to individuals who request for the same by submitting their demographic and biometric information, by undergoing the process of enrolment.
- To develop policy, procedure and systems for Aadhaar number holders for updating and authenticating their digital identity.
- Ensure availability, scalability and resilience of the Aadhaar technology infrastructure.
- Build a strong and robust organization to carry forward the vision and values of the UIDAI.
- To ensure security and confidentiality of identity information and authentication records of individuals.
- To ensure the compliance of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 ("Aadhaar Act, 2016"), as amended, by all individuals and agencies in letter and spirit.
- To make regulations & rules consistent with the Aadhaar Act, 2016 (as amended), for implementing the provisions of the Aadhaar Act, 2016 (as amended).

## 4. Stakeholders

| Sl. No | Stakeholder | Description |

## 5. Our Services

| Sl. No | Service | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aadhaar Enrolment | The process, as may be specified by Regulations, to collect demographic and biometric information from individuals by the enrolling agencies for the purpose of issuing an Aadhaar number to such individual under the Aadhaar Act 2016. |
| 2 | Aadhaar Generation | Aadhaar Generation involves processes like quality check, packet validation, demographic and biometric de-duplication etc. Aadhaar is generated successfully only if: the quality of enrolment data meets prescribed standards laid down by UIDAI; the enrolment packet passes all the validations done in CIDR; no demographic/biometric duplicate is found. |
| 3 | Aadhaar Letter / Card Delivery / e-Aadhaar | UIDAI, through its logistics partner(s), delivers printed Aadhaar letters/cards to Aadhaar number holders. It also provides a web-based solution to download a digitally signed soft copy of the Aadhaar letter, also called e-Aadhaar. |
| 4 | Aadhaar Update | This service has been created to facilitate Aadhaar number holders to update their demographic and biometric data in Aadhaar. |
| 5 | Authentication facility or Service | "Authentication" means the process by which the Aadhaar number, along with demographic information or biometric information of an individual, is submitted to the Central Identities Data Repository for its verification, and such Repository verifies the correctness, or the lack thereof, on the basis of information available with it. "Authentication facility" means the facility provided by the Authority for authenticating the Aadhaar number along with demographic or biometric information of an Aadhaar number holder through the process of authentication, by providing a Yes/No response or e-KYC data, as applicable. Types of Aadhaar Authentication Facilities for Aadhaar number holders include: (a) Online based – request for authentication is submitted online; (b) Offline based – via QR Code, e-Aadhaar verification, paperless and paper-based verification. |
| 6 | e-KYC authentication facility or e-KYC service | "e-KYC authentication facility" means a type of authentication facility in which the biometric information and/or OTP and Aadhaar number, securely submitted with the consent of the Aadhaar number holder through a requesting entity, is matched against the data available in the CIDR, and the Authority returns a digitally signed response containing e-KYC data along with other technical details related to the authentication transaction. |
| 7 | Aadhaar Document Update Service | Aadhaar number holders may, on completion of every 10 years from the date of enrolment for Aadhaar, update their supporting documents in Aadhaar at least once, by submitting Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of Address (POA) documents as specified, so as to ensure continued accuracy of information in the Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR), in such manner as may be specified by the Authority from time to time. |

## 6. Standards of Services provided by UIDAI

### 6.1 Aadhaar Enrolment

| Sl. No | Service | Success Indicator | Performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aadhaar Enrolment | Up to 20 minutes after start of enrolment | 95% |

**A. Process for Enrolment**

Enrolment is conducted by Enrolment Agencies working on behalf of Registrars. One of the following approaches may be adopted to enrol an individual seeking to enrol at the enrolment centre:

1. **Document based Enrolment** — the individual seeking to enrol has to submit one valid Proof of Identity (PoI) and one valid Proof of Address (PoA).
2. **Head of Family (HoF) based Enrolment** — the Head of Family may introduce family members by means of documents which establish the Proof of Relationship (PoR). For HoF-based enrolment, the HoF must be 18 years or more in age and be the mother, father or legal guardian of the applicant. Name and Aadhaar number of one of the parents/legal guardian is to be provided for a child between the age of 5–18 for HoF-based enrolment. Biometrics of the parent/guardian will also be required to authenticate the transaction.

The above approaches require successful capture of biometric information (facial image, all 10 fingerprints and scans of both irises) and demographic information like name, date of birth, gender, residential address, mobile number (optional) and email address (optional).

**B. Child Enrolment for children below five years of age**

1. **Document based Enrolment** — for children below five years of age, the following demographic and biometric information shall be collected:
   a. Name
   b. Date of Birth
   c. Gender
   d. Email (mandatory in case of NRI child)
   e. Mobile number (optional)
   f. Facial image of the child shall be captured
   g. Proof of Identity (POI), Proof of Address (POA), Proof of Date of Birth (PDB) (optional) as type of documents to be presented.
2. **HoF-based enrolment** — for children below five years of age, the following demographic and biometric information shall be collected:
   a. Name
   b. Gender
   c. Date of Birth
   d. Email (mandatory for NRI)
   e. Phone number (optional)
   f. Aadhaar number of mother and/or father of the child (if details of only one parent are given, whether the other parent is currently not residing with the child will also be captured) or legal guardian; relationship with the child will also be recorded.
   g. The address of such a child shall be the same as that of the linked parent/guardian.
   h. Facial image of the child shall be captured. To authenticate the transaction, the biometric information of any one parent/guardian shall be captured.
   i. The Proof of Relationship (PoR) document establishing the relationship between the linked parent/guardian and the child shall be collected at the time of enrolment. Only those children whose names are recorded in the relationship document (PoR) can be enrolled based on it.

- List of acceptable supporting documents: https://uidai.gov.in/images/commdoc/List_of_Supporting_Document_for_Aadhaar_Enrolment_and_Update.pdf
- Original documents must be presented at the time of enrolment. They shall be returned after making an electronic copy and verifying it against the originals.
- Separate Aadhaar Enrolment/Update forms (by age and residential status) can be checked/downloaded from https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar/downloads/enrolment-and-update-forms.html
- The individual seeking to enrol may visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre and submit the information/documents to the operator. The applicant should check and confirm the details entered by the operator at the centre and sign the enrolment slip generated by the operator.
- Aadhaar Enrolment Centres can be located at https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/

**C. Exception Process for Enrolment**

In case of biometric exception (unavailability of any of 10 fingers or 2 irises), an additional exception photograph (highlighting the exception) is taken in addition to the photograph of the face. If automatic biometric capture does not happen, the operator should force-capture them in the enrolment software.

**Note:**
- Aadhaar enrolment is free of cost.
- The Aadhaar Enrolment/Update form is also free of cost. The applicant can take a printout of the respective form from the UIDAI website and submit it at the time of enrolment.
- An individual seeking to enrol should enrol only once. If the request is rejected, they may re-enrol unless advised otherwise by UIDAI.

### 6.2 Aadhaar Generation

| Sl. No | Service | Description | Success Indicator | Service Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aadhaar Generation | Aadhaar is generated successfully if: (1) quality of enrolment data meets prescribed standards laid down by UIDAI; (2) the enrolment packet passes all the validations done in the Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR); (3) no demographic/biometric duplicate is found; (4) there are no unforeseen technical issues; (5) in case of adults, verification may be conducted through the State/UT Authorities concerned before Aadhaar generation. If any of the above conditions is not satisfied, Aadhaar generation may be put on hold and generation/rejection may take longer. | **Children (0–18 yrs):** normally up to 30 days from the date of enrolment\*. **Adults (18+):** normally up to 180 days from the date of enrolment\*. | 90% |

They may also check the status by: (I) visiting the myAadhaar portal https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/CheckAadhaarStatus; (II) calling 1947 (contact centre) or emailing help@uidai.gov.in. | Normally up to 30 days from the date of enrolment\* | 90% |

\* Registrars/Enrolment Agencies are normally expected to send the enrolment packet to UIDAI within 2 days of enrolment.

### 6.4 Demographic and Biometric Update Service

| Sl. No | Service Offering | Success Indicator | Service Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demographic Update (A, B) | Normally up to 30 days from the date of update by the Aadhaar number holder | 90% |
| 1 | HoF-based Address Update (C) | Normally up to 60 days from the date of update, including the 30-day HoF acceptance period | 90% |
| 2 | Biometric Update | Normally up to 30 days from the date of update | 90% |
| 3 | Exception Handling | Normally up to 90 days from the date of update | 90% |

**1. Demographic Update**

**A.** An Aadhaar number holder may seek update/correction of the following demographic details at the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre after submitting documents as per the list of acceptable documents:

a. Name (can be updated twice)
b. Gender (can be updated once)
c. Date of Birth (can be updated once)
d. Address
e. Mobile Number
f. Email

Separate Aadhaar enrolment/update forms (by age and residential status) can be checked/downloaded from https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar/downloads/enrolment-and-update-forms.html

**B.** Aadhaar number holders who have registered a mobile number in Aadhaar can also update the address in their Aadhaar through the online portal myAadhaar (https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in).

**C. HoF-based Address Update.** An Aadhaar number holder can update the address in Aadhaar with the consent of their Head of Family (HoF), by visiting an enrolment centre or through myAadhaar (https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in).

- *At an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre:* the Aadhaar number holder submits a Proof of Relationship document (such as Ration Card, Marksheet, Marriage Certificate or Passport) mentioning the names of both the applicant and the HoF and the relationship between them. The HoF must be physically present, as their biometric authentication is required to complete the transaction.
- *Online:* the Aadhaar number holder submits the PoR document through myAadhaar. The HoF validates the request with OTP-based authentication. Process video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WutM30q61VM
- *Note:* In online HoF-based address update, if the HoF refuses to share their address or does not accept within 30 days of SRN creation, the request is automatically closed and the applicant is informed by SMS. If the request is closed or rejected because the HoF did not accept, or is rejected during processing, the service fee is **not refunded**.
- The HoF must be 18 years or older. For applicants aged 18 and above, the HoF can be the applicant's mother, father, legal guardian, spouse, child/ward or sibling. For applicants below 18, the HoF can be the mother, father or legal guardian.
- List of acceptable supporting documents: https://uidai.gov.in/images/commdoc/List_of_Supporting_Document_for_Aadhaar_Enrolment_and_Update.pdf
- Enrolment/update forms: https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar/downloads/enrolment-and-update-forms.html

**2. Biometric Update**

The Aadhaar number holder has to go to an Aadhaar Enrolment Centre for biometric data update under the following condition:

- A child who attains the age of 5 or 15 years requires a **Mandatory Biometric Update**, which is free of charge if done between the ages of 5–7 years and 15–17 years.

The Aadhaar number holder may also opt for a biometric update in the following conditions:

i. Events like accidents or diseases leading to biometric exception conditions (loss of limbs, loss of eyes, etc.)
ii. If there is an authentication issue
iii. To update any biometric information, including photo.

Enrolment centres can be located at https://bhuvan-app3.nrsc.gov.in/aadhaar/

**3. Exception Handling**

To avoid misuse or malpractice, limits have been set for updating demographic information in Aadhaar:

- **Name — can be updated twice.** A third name update can be done through exception handling: carry out the update at an Aadhaar Centre, then approach the concerned UIDAI Regional Office for approval. SOP: https://www.uidai.gov.in//images/SOP_dated_28-10-2021-Name_and_Gender_update_request_under_exception_handling_process_Circular_dated_03-11-2021.pdf
- **Gender — once.** A second gender update can be done through exception handling, in the same way (same SOP as above).
- **Date of Birth — once.** A second DoB update can be done through the exception handling process: carry out the update at an Aadhaar Centre, then approach the concerned Regional Office for approval. SOP: https://uidai.gov.in/images/SOP_for_DOB_update.pdf (a revised DoB SOP was issued on 24 Dec 2025, see uidai.gov.in → UIDAI Documents)
- The Aadhaar number holder can update their information by enrolling at the nearest Enrolment Centre with a valid document.
- Once a request is rejected for exceeding the limits, the Aadhaar number holder shall/may be required to approach the Regional Office (RO) of their region by calling 1947 or emailing help@uidai.gov.in, with details of the update and the necessary supporting documents.
- The RO carries out due diligence to check whether the request beyond the threshold is genuine, and may seek additional information or carry out a field investigation.
- If the RO finds the request genuine, the case is sent to the Tech Centre for processing/re-processing.
