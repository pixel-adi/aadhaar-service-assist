# Lists of Acceptable Documents for Aadhaar Enrolment and Update (2026)

> **Source:** Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) First Amendment Regulations, 2026, notified 06.05.2026 (Gazette) and in force from publication. UIDAI's own "List of Acceptable Document for Aadhaar Enrolment and Update" PDF was uploaded to uidai.gov.in → Enrolment & Updates on 11-06-2026.
> **Caution:** Prepared on 17 Nov 2026 from a condensed reproduction of the notification. Row order and ticks follow it, and document names are lightly shortened. Before giving a final yes/no on an unusual document, check the official PDF.
> **Legend:** ✔ = accepted for that proof · ✘ = not accepted · # = date-of-birth rules apply (see List IV note d) · \* = PoA documents from this list also apply.
> **PoI** = Proof of Identity (name + photo) · **PoA** = Proof of Address (name + address in India) · **PoR** = Proof of Relationship (applicant's + HoF's names) · **PDB** = Proof of Date of Birth.

## Which list to use

| Situation | List |
|---|---|
| **Updating** an existing Aadhaar (any age): address, name, DoB, gender, document refresh | **List IV**. Use this for almost every question. |
| New enrolment, age 18+ | List III |
| New enrolment, age 5 to under 18 | List II |
| New enrolment, below 5 | List I |

### Notes to List IV

**(a) What makes a document acceptable.** It must be currently valid; the person must be entitled to it; its information must be verifiable from the issuing source; and the issuing authority must not have declared it not to be proof for that purpose.

**(b) Criteria.**
- A PoI must show at least the name and photograph.
- A PoR must show at least the applicant's name and the HoF's name.
- PoI, PoA, PoR and PDB documents must be in the name of the person updating.
- Name and DoB must match across all PoI, PoR and PDB documents. The name is copied exactly as written in the document, with nothing added.

**(c) HoF-based update.** The HoF must already have a valid Aadhaar. Either parent or the legal guardian can be the HoF for a minor. Both parents' Aadhaar numbers are needed, or only the single parent's or guardian's if that applies. Biometric authentication by one parent or guardian is mandatory. The individual's address is taken from the HoF's Aadhaar.

**(d) Date-of-birth updates (#).**
- If the DoB was declared or approximate: residents **under 18** must submit a **birth certificate**; NRIs under 18 may give a birth certificate or Indian passport; those **18 and over** may give any acceptable PDB document.
- If a birth certificate was submitted earlier, a **corrected birth certificate with the same Birth Registration Number** is needed.
- If another DoB document was submitted earlier, a corrected version of that document or a birth certificate is needed.
- Women whose name changed after marriage may use pre-marriage DoB documents together with proof of the name change.
- DoB updates follow UIDAI's DoB SOP (latest issued 24 Dec 2025).

**(e) Name and gender beyond the limits** in Regulation 19(3) (name twice, gender once) follow a separate exception process via the Regional Office.

**(f) Children (0–18) recorded as "Baby of …":** the first full-name update needs a birth certificate.

**(g) HoF-based name update for minors (under 18):** surname changes, phonetic spelling corrections, and expanding or abbreviating are allowed if the primary name stays the same. A birth certificate or guardianship document showing the revised name is needed.

**(h) HoF-based address update** can be used when the person has **no PoA document** in their own name.

## General conditions (all lists)

- Documents must be currently valid, the holder must be entitled to them, and their details must be verifiable at the source.
- The issuer must not have declared the document invalid as proof for that purpose.
- All proofs must be in the name of the person enrolling or updating.
- Name and DoB must be consistent across the PoI, PoR and PDB documents.
- Originals are shown at the centre and returned after scanning. For online uploads, scans must be clear (JPEG/PNG/PDF, under 2 MB each on myAadhaar).
- Aadhaar validity for foreign nationals: 10 years for OCI and Nepal/Bhutan; until visa or permit expiry for others; until LTV expiry for LTV holders.
